Job Title: Junior Full Stack Developer Intern

About the Role:
We are looking for an enthusiastic intern to join our product engineering
team. You will work alongside senior engineers to design, build, and ship
features across our web application.

Requirements:
- Must have strong experience with React and Node.js.
- Required: Proficient in MongoDB database design.
- Must be able to build and consume REST API services.
- Good knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript fundamentals.
- Familiarity with Git and version control workflows.

Preferred:
- Familiarity with Docker and AWS.
- Exposure to TypeScript or Express.
- Contributions to open-source or personal projects.

Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain frontend components with React.
- Implement backend endpoints and integrate third-party APIs.
- Participate in code reviews and team standups.
- Write unit tests and clear documentation for your features.

Internship Details:
- Duration: 6 months, in-person (Bengaluru).
- Stipend: Competitive, based on demonstrated skill.
